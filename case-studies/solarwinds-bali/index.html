SolarWinds Transform Partner Summit APJ 2024 — Bali

Transforming Partnerships in Paradise

A three-day regional summit uniting partners from across Asia Pacific and Japan — combining business focus with Balinese warmth and flawless event precision.

Challenge

SolarWinds sought to host its flagship APJ Partner Summit as an immersive experience that both celebrated top partners and strengthened regional alignment.

Solution

NowDMC managed end-to-end destination logistics and event operations for more than 140 regional attendees at the Grand Hyatt Bali. Guests were greeted with cold towels, fresh juices, and traditional Balinese welcomes before a buffet dinner, gamelan performance, and fire dance spectacle.

Result

A three-day programme bringing regional partners together for business sessions, hospitality and cultural performances.
