Appsflyer “Connect” Event — Bangkok

Reconnecting in Style at the Conrad Bangkok

After a four-year hiatus, Appsflyer’s “Connect” celebrated reconnection and engagement — blending business insights with uniquely Thai experiences across four days.

Situation

Appsflyer wanted a memorable return to onsite gatherings. With the theme “Connect,” the brief was to celebrate reconnections and drive meaningful interaction.

Our Mission

Deliver end-to-end event management for 320 attendees at the Conrad Bangkok — from concept and production to guest experiences and gala night.

Our Kinda Different Approach

We ran full production (lighting, sound, décor), two rooftop evenings with karaoke and live bands, four cultural excursions, and a luxury gala awards dinner. We also handled creative development, video editing, cross-country coordination, and interactive engagement.

Services Provided

  • End-to-end event & production management
  • Venue liaison & stage design
  • Audio, lighting & AV
  • Creative concept, branding & video editing
  • Entertainment (live bands & cultural performances)
  • Delegate experiences: cultural visits, cooking class & Muay Thai
  • Gala dinner planning & awards coordination
  • Regional coordination with Appsflyer APAC teams

What We Achieved

A four-day return to in-person gatherings, with conference production, networking, cultural excursions and a gala awards dinner.
